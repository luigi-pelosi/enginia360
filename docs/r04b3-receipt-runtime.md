Receipt type is **idDocumentType=0**, independently observed in the retained standalone sent request and exact detail; the old type5 executor brief was an assistant handoff error, not a domain decision. Type5 is refused. R05.a.8/a.9 source projections, ownership, hashes and acquisition incidents remain immutable; no new sandbox proof is required.

Secret token bytes/path are not fingerprint material. Receipt settings are excluded from existing sales/purchase fingerprints. Wrong/missing/changed binding holds without retargeting or backfill. Historical missing bindings are not repaired automatically.

A separate fenced Integrations worker persists uncertainty **before** business POST. A known ACK is durably stored and only exact GET detail can confirm type/vendor/reference/document number/BU/numerator and row identity/description/quantity/frozen price/warehouse/VAT. Receipt quantity `0.123456` and frozen price `12.345678` are compared independently, not inferred from totals. Only the observed single-row/destination/VAT shape is supported; no fiscal validation or exactly-once guarantee.

Lost ACK, timeout, HTTP500, empty/full/nonunique/mismatching lookup and malformed responses remain HOLD/GET-only; **no blind POST retry**. Even a synthetic positive unknown-ACK lookup is not promoted into proven recovery. Preflight NotSent may return to queued because no business POST left the process. Known ACK detail recovery is distinct from provider idempotency or conclusive absence, neither of which was observed. Existing GiobbyWire enforces verified identity/company, one RAM-frozen host token per identity/business sequence, trusted TLS/allowlist, no redirects, byte/body deadlines and bounded worker lease. Automated login/token refresh remains out of scope.

D86 overlay is **non-authoritative** with explicit internal confirmed-source/frozen-line link, not ERP-native linkage. ERP per-order residual remains UNKNOWN. API flags preserve the visible stock discrepancy: receipt `0.123456` versus stock report increment `0.12346`; no silent stock correction/local stock writer.
